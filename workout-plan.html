Saturday — Bike Ride 🚴‍♀️

30–60 min
Why this day
  • Active recovery for the legs
  • Pumps calves → boosts circulation
  • Low-impact, no joint strain
  • 48 hrs after upper body, 36 hrs before next leg day
Option 1 — Steady Ride (default)
30–45 min at conversational pace.
Heart rate ~60–70% of max. Best for circulation, lymphatic flow, and fat burn. Should feel like you can talk in short sentences but not sing.
Option 2 — Interval Ride (when short on time)
PhaseDurationEffort
Warmup5 minEasy
Hard intervals × 81 minHard
Recovery × 81 minEasy
Cooldown5 minEasy
💡 If legs feel puffy after a long ride: elevate them 10–15 min, or wear compression socks during the ride.

⏱ Tight on time? Do these first

Lower body day Squat → Deadlift → Hip thrust → Split squat
Upper body day Chest press → Row → Shoulder press → Lat pulldown → Arms
Full body day Deadlift → Squat → Push-up → Row → Plank

Key Principles

  • Last 2 reps of each set should feel challenging
  • Form over weight — especially weeks 1–6
  • Add a rep or small weight increase each week
  • Track workouts in a notes app
  • Intensity > duration: work hard during these 30 min
  • Rest 1 day between gym sessions
